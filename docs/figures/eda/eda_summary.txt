==============================================================================
8. KEY FINDINGS
==============================================================================
  1. The dataset has 5,882 turns in 10 long conversations (369-689 turns each, over 272 sessions): far too long to paste into a prompt.
  2. Turn length is right-skewed (median 20 words, mean 22.7, skew 1.09); only 0.9% of turns have fewer than 4 words.
  3. The raw data is clean (0 missing texts, 0 duplicate ids), so cleaning has to happen at memory time: in conv-26, 65 of the first 100 turns were filtered out as small talk.
  4. 158 turns (2.7%) are length outliers; they are real long messages and are kept because they carry many facts.
  5. Multi-hop questions need 3.1 evidence turns on average vs 1.1 for single-hop, which explains why they are the hardest for retrieval-based memory.
  6. Messages get shorter as a session goes on (words vs position in session r = -0.33), but length is unrelated to where a turn sits in the whole conversation (r = -0.00); length features (words, chars) are almost identical (r = 0.99), so one of them is enough when modelling.
